What this site collects

Nothing. This site has no accounts, no analytics, no cookies and no server-side forms. Anything you type into a booking or inquiry box stays in your own browser until you choose to send it.

What happens when you tap a WhatsApp button

Tapping a booking or inquiry button opens WhatsApp (wa.me/6583803422) with a pre-filled message. The message is sent only if you press send inside WhatsApp. Your name, chosen treatment, date, time and notes are then shared with Passion Spa via WhatsApp, under WhatsApp’s own privacy terms — not stored by this site.
